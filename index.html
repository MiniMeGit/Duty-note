Duty quick note

Turns your roster screenshots and ATC flight plans into one note page, with labelled blank space for everything you write by hand during the duty.

1Roster screenshots

Flight Details for each sector in flying order, taken without scrolling, plus the crew list and any Pax Info screens (put each right after its sector). Screen type and registration are read automatically; correct the registration here if it is wrong and the tool learns the letters.

2ATC flight plans

Paste one or more FPL messages. They are matched to sectors in order. The REG item names each aircraft.

3Handwriting fields

Edit labels (saved on this iPad)

One label per line. Sector labels fill the space beside the flight card first, the rest go below it.

Everything is processed on this device. Screenshots and flight plans are never uploaded.
